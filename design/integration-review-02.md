# Integration Review 02 — Personality Contrast Speedplay (Bartle's Achiever & the Cautious Roleplayer)

### Method

Round 1 (review 01) played the Inquisitive Investigator. This round plays the
two personalities the design bible names alongside it (arcs §2.4), through the
real App UI (jsdom click-through, seeded RNG):

1. **The Tryhard (Bartle's Achiever)** — files every anomaly clean for maximum
   payout, buys out the terminal the moment funds allow, weighs every choice by
   gate value (Doubt/Perception > Salary > Routine; Attention = 0 because
   nothing consumes it), and walks into telegraphed deaths if they pay. 12 nights.
2. **The Cautious Roleplayer** — never logs a discrepancy, declines every
   curious choice, buys only cozy goods, keeps the head down. 6 nights.

(Bartle's taxonomy: achievers "prioritize gaining points, reaching levels, and
achieving goals to demonstrate mastery" — the min-maxer lens this game's
30-shift floor is tuned for.)

### Findings and dispositions

1. **Death was free — retreat strictly dominated.** The lethal choice paid the
   same Component as backing off, plus more qualities and an Attention reset.
   P1/P3/§6 price a death at an hour of the next shift's budget. *Fixed: a
   termination now docks six actions from the tank and says so in the
   logbook; the tryhard's post-death tank dropped 50 → 37.*
2. **The cap advertised phantom rewards.** From Night 3 every button still
   read "+1 Doubt" while the file stayed at 5 — a promise the game never pays.
   *Fixed: effect previews are cap-aware; a gain the ceiling absorbs renders
   "Doubt MAX" (choice, filing preview, and outcome line).*
3. **The Achiever's game is over on Night 4.** All goods owned, both gate
   qualities capped, one Component held. The remaining lever is an uncapped
   credit total with no sink. *Not code-fixable: this is the Month-1 content
   gap (arcs §2.3 tracks, Arc II), flagged here again for the record. The MAX
   display at least makes the exhaustion legible instead of lying.*
4. **The incurious loop had no pull.** Six nights at tier 0 with zero Doubt
   and zero guidance that noticing is the on-ramp. *Fixed: from Night 3, M.
   prods an Unknown Operator with no Doubt — "Noticing is permitted — the
   button has been on your screen all along." One logged discrepancy ends it.*
5. **The shop sold a sealed board's promise.** Supply cards promised notices
   a tier-0 operator cannot reach. *Fixed: cards now label the board as sealed
   until the system recognizes you; the purchase remains possible but honest.*
6. **The orientation waiver was strictly dominated.** Skipping forfeited the
   break-room qualities with nothing in return. *Fixed: the waiver files
   Routine +2 — prior knowledge accepted as compliance.*
7. **Still true from round 1, still content not tuning:** Attention pegs with
   no consumer; deaths have no Patch Scar/Reinstatement yet (the hour dock is
   the first honest installment); the routine pool's promised refill does not
   exist; the true Day-30 floor awaits the Month-1 tracks.
