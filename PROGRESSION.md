# FALSE REALITY — Story Progression & Promotion System

## Overview

This document outlines the player's journey through FALSE REALITY, from their first night as an unknown operator to their final choice at 0600. The progression is both narrative (story beats, revelations) and mechanical (promotions, resources, abilities).

---

## The Three Arcs

### Arc I: THE ROSTER (Days 1-9)
**Theme:** "It's just a job."
**Player Question:** "Why does everything feel... wrong?"

**Story Beats:**
1. **First Shift** (Day 1) — Tutorial, orientation, first tasks
2. **Routine** (Days 2-4) — Learn the job, notice small wrongnesses
3. **Coincidences** (Days 5-8) — Day crew notes change, Building 7 work orders
4. **The First Death** (Day 9) — Scripted death, discover the loop exists

**Promotion:** 
- **Start:** Unknown Operator (no name, no history)
- **End:** Operator (acknowledged by system, has a file)

**Resources Gained:**
- Salary: ~200-400 (from routine work)
- Perception: +1 (noticed the wrongness)
- Doubt: +1 (questioned the routine)
- First Checklist Item: The Key (from Floor 12 expedition)

**Abilities Unlocked:**
- Basic task execution
- Access to break room
- Reading memos
- First Notice storylets (Perception gates)

---

### Arc II: THE PATCH (Days 10-42)
**Theme:** "Every truth costs a Tuesday."
**Player Question:** "What is this place, and what am I?"

**Story Beats:**
1. **Evidence** (Days 10-15) — Test the loop, discover what persists
2. **The Others** (Days 16-30) — Meet VANTABLACK, The Cleaner, learn of Operator 5
3. **The Truth** (Days 31-42) — Learn you are the exception handler, the Manager's offer

**Promotions:**
- **Day 10:** Senior Operator (access to restricted areas)
- **Day 20:** Lead Operator (can dispatch yourself)
- **Day 30:** Acting Manager (can read classified memos)
- **Day 42:** The Summons (Manager offers you a choice)

**Resources Gained:**
- Salary: ~2,000-3,000 (cumulative)
- Components: 4/5 (Key, Lens, Wire, Crystal, Chip)
- Doubt: +3 (understand the loop)
- Perception: +2 (see the patterns)
- Residue: +5 (permanent memories)
- Checklist Items: 4/5 (Key, Signal, Gap, Name*)

*Note: The Name can be recovered from Operator 5's logbook OR given by the Manager

**Abilities Unlocked:**
- Access to secret zones (Floor 12, Records Basement, Vent Network, Rooftop Array, Off-Map Sectors)
- Investigate glitches (spend actions to dig deeper)
- Use the logbook (persistent notes)
- Listen to VANTABLACK's transmissions
- Read Operator 5's logbook pages
- Die and return (The Reinstatement)

---

### Arc III: 0600 (Days 43-52)
**Theme:** "The hour that kills you."
**Player Question:** "How do I make it stop?"

**Story Beats:**
1. **The Summons** (Days 43-45) — Meet the Manager, receive the offer
2. **Final Preparation** (Days 46-48) — Gather last component, recover name
3. **The Last Shift** (Days 49-52) — No routine, only story, the finale

**Promotions:**
- **Day 43:** Manager (if you accept) OR Rogue Operator (if you refuse)
- **Day 52:** [ENDING]

**Resources Gained:**
- Salary: ~3,500-4,500 (cumulative)
- Components: 5/5 (all gathered)
- Sixth Component: The Interim (from death or Cleaner's gift)
- Doubt: MAX (you understand everything)
- Residue: MAX (you remember everything)

**Abilities Unlocked:**
- Assemble the Seam Ripper
- Survive past 0600 (if you have the tool)
- Crack the Loom's code
- Choose your ending

## The Promotion System

### What is a Promotion?

A promotion is a **narrative rank advancement** that:
1. Changes your title in the system
2. Unlocks new story content
3. Grants new abilities
4. Increases your Salary cap
5. Changes how NPCs treat you

### Promotion Tiers

| Tier | Title | Days | Unlocks |
|------|-------|------|---------|
| 0 | Unknown Operator | 1-9 | Basic tasks, break room, memos |
| 1 | Operator | 9-15 | Notice storylets, first investigation |
| 2 | Senior Operator | 15-20 | Restricted areas, deeper investigation |
| 3 | Lead Operator | 20-30 | Self-dispatch, access to Operator 5's log |
| 4 | Acting Manager | 30-42 | Classified memos, all secret zones |
| 5 | Manager / Rogue | 42-52 | The Summons, final choice, endings |

### How Promotions Work

Promotions are **story-gated**, not grind-gated. You advance by:
- Completing key story beats
- Making significant discoveries
- Surviving major events (deaths)
- Reaching narrative milestones

**Example:** You become "Senior Operator" after discovering that the logbook persists across loops. The system recognizes your competence and grants you access to restricted areas.

## Resource System

### Primary Resources

#### 1. Credits (Salary)
**Display:** Top bar, right side
**Icon:** ¤ (currency symbol)
**Source:** Routine work (task execution)
**Cap:** Increases with promotion
- Unknown Operator: 500
- Operator: 1,000
- Senior Operator: 2,500
- Lead Operator: 5,000
- Acting Manager: 10,000
- Manager: ∞

**Uses:**
- Buy supplies (bolt cutters, torch, bribes)
- Cool Attention (bribe the system)
- Purchase conveniences (faster routing, batch actions)
- Never gates story progression

**Starting Amount:** 0
**Typical Gain:** 5-15 per task
**Total Over Game:** ~3,500-4,500

#### 2. Components (Story Resources)
**Display:** Profile page, inventory section
**Icon:** ⚙ (gear/component symbol)
**Source:** Secret zones (one-time discovery)
**Cap:** 6 (Key, Lens, Wire, Crystal, Chip, Interim)

**Components:**
1. **NULL KEY** — Opens doors the map doesn't have (Floor 12)
2. **LENS** — Read the world's source text (Records Basement)
3. **WIRE** — Connective tissue, Operator 5's route (Vent Network)
4. **CRYSTAL** — Tunes VANTABLACK's signal (Rooftop Array)
5. **CHIP** — Persistence, copy of you (Off-Map Sectors)
6. **INTERIM** — The sixth component (The Interim, death or Cleaner's gift)

**Uses:**
- Assemble the Seam Ripper (endgame tool)
- Authorize actions in the finale
- Unlock special story paths
- Never grindable, always story-gated

#### 3. Attention (Hidden Heat Meter)
**Display:** Subtle visual cues only (memo tone, Auditor sightings)
**Icon:** None (hidden by design)
**Source:** Investigation, non-routine actions, asking questions
**Cap:** 10 (death at 10)

**Mechanics:**
- Cooled by routine work
- Heated by investigation
- Visible through system tone (polite → suspicious → threatening)
- At 10: The Auditor appears, you die
- Resets on death (but floor increases)

**Player Awareness:**
- Arc I: Hidden, only felt through tone
- Arc II: Semi-visible (performance review memos)
- Arc III: More visible (Auditor sightings)

#### 4. Doubt (Understanding Meter)
**Display:** Profile page, stats section
**Icon:** ? (question mark)
**Source:** Discoveries, reading logs, investigating
**Cap:** 5

**Levels:**
- 0: Don't know anything is wrong
- 1: Noticed wrongness (Arc I end)
- 2: Know the loop exists (Arc II start)
- 3: Understand the patch (Arc II mid)
- 4: Know what you are (Arc II end)
- 5: Understand everything (Arc III end)

**Unlocks:**
- 1: Notice storylets
- 2: Investigation actions
- 3: Access to Operator 5's log
- 4: All secret zones
- 5: The Summons, final choice

#### 5. Perception (Observation Meter)
**Display:** Profile page, stats section
**Icon:** 👁 (eye)
**Source:** Noticing details, reading carefully
**Cap:** 5

**Levels:**
- 0: Miss everything
- 1: Notice obvious wrongness
- 2: See patterns
- 3: Read between the lines
- 4: Spot hidden details
- 5: See the truth

**Unlocks:**
- 1: Basic Notice storylets
- 2: Hidden memos
- 3: Operator 5's clues
- 4: VANTABLACK's true nature
- 5: The Cleaner's identity

#### 6. Residue (Permanent Memory)
**Display:** Profile page, logbook section
**Icon:** 📖 (book)
**Source:** Major discoveries, deaths, revelations
**Cap:** ∞ (accumulates over game)

**What It Tracks:**
- Number of deaths
- Major discoveries made
- People met
- Secrets learned
- Checklist items gathered

**Uses:**
- Unlocks special dialogue
- Changes NPC reactions
- Affects ending availability
- The diegetic save file

## The Checklist (Endgame Progression)

The Checklist is the **diegetic quest log** — five items that gate the escape ending.

| # | Item | Where to Find | When Available |
|---|------|---------------|----------------|
| 1 | **The Key** | Floor 12 expedition | Day 9 (Arc I end) |
| 2 | **The Lens** | Records Basement | Day 15-20 (Arc II early) |
| 3 | **The Wire** | Vent Network | Day 20-25 (Arc II mid) |
| 4 | **The Crystal** | Rooftop Array | Day 25-30 (Arc II mid) |
| 5 | **The Chip** | Off-Map Sectors | Day 30-35 (Arc II late) |
| 6 | **The Interim** | The Interim (death) or Cleaner's gift | Day 35-42 (Arc II end) |

**Display:** Profile page, checklist section
**Icon:** ✓ (checkmark for gathered, □ for missing)

**Progression:**
- Day 9: 1/6 (Key)
- Day 20: 3/6 (Key, Lens, Wire)
- Day 30: 4/6 (Key, Lens, Wire, Crystal)
- Day 35: 5/6 (Key, Lens, Wire, Crystal, Chip)
- Day 42: 6/6 (all gathered)

## Quick Resource Bar (UI Element)

### Location
**Option A:** Top bar (above main content)
**Option B:** Bottom bar (below main content)
**Recommendation:** Top bar (always visible, matches terminal aesthetic)

### Layout

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ FALSE REALITY  │  [HOME] [DIRECTIVE] [GRID] [SHIFT] [BULLETIN]  │

```

### What It Shows

| Element | Display | Tooltip |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Credits | ¤ 2,340 | "Currency from routine work" |
| Components | ⚙ 4/6 | "Story resources gathered" |
| Day | Day 28 | "Current shift day" |
| Tasks | 42/50 | "Tasks completed this shift" |
| Profile | [PROFILE] | "View full operator profile" |

### Why These Elements?

- **Credits:** Most frequently used resource, player needs to track spending
- **Components:** Tracks endgame progression, motivating exploration
- **Day:** Narrative progress, how far through the story
- **Tasks:** Current shift progress, how much time left
- **Profile:** Quick access to full stats, logbook, checklist

## Summary

The progression system in FALSE REALITY is **narrative-first, mechanical-second**. Players advance through the story by making discoveries and surviving events, not by grinding. Resources track progress but never gate the core narrative. The promotion system reflects the player's growing understanding and competence within the simulation.

**Key Principles:**
1. Story gates everything, not resources
2. Resources track progress, not gate it
3. Promotions are narrative milestones, not grind rewards
4. The UI reinforces the diegetic fiction (you're an operator, not a player)
5. Every mechanic serves the theme (monotony, discovery, choice)

**Player Journey:**
- Start: Unknown, confused, routine
- Middle: Curious, investigating, dying, learning
- End: Understanding, prepared, choosing

**The game is not about getting stronger. It's about understanding what you are.**
